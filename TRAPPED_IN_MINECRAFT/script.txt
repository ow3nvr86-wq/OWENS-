You wake up trapped in Minecraft. No tools, no food, and about ten minutes until dark.
So you punch a tree. Bare-handed. It works... and the top of the tree just floats there.
You make a pickaxe and mine a mountain. Sixty-four blocks of stone go in your pocket. Over a hundred and fifty tonnes. You don't even notice.
The sun's going down. You slap together a house. Four walls, a door, no windows. Windows are for people who aren't scared.
You made it. You climb into bed...
...and through the one gap you forgot to fill, two glowing white eyes are watching you.
Herobrine. The guy the game swears it removed.
