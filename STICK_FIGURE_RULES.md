# Stick-figure Shorts - house rules (asked for after "The Odyssey in 45 Seconds")

Apply all of these to every stick-figure (and cartoon) Short from now on:

1. **60 fps.** Render and deliver at 60 fps (YouTube Shorts plays it). Word times stay in seconds.
2. **Motion blur.** Each output frame is the average of 4 sub-frames spread across the frame's shutter.
3. **Ease every change - no snaps.** Expressions blend over 3-6 frames; transformations (poofs, costume swaps)
   squash, pop and settle; props are handed over, not swapped; every action has anticipation -> move -> overshoot -> settle.
4. **Fewer, longer shots.** About 10-12 setups for 40-45 s (nothing under ~1.5 s). Montages are one continuous
   camera move across a long panorama. Use whips sparingly (3-4 per video); prefer match cuts and camera moves.
5. **Better walks.** Feet planted (no sliding: travel speed = stride x cadence), a bounce on each step, secondary
   motion on crests, beards, hair, cloaks and props (follow-through and drag).
6. **Calmer finish.** Light, slow-changing grain (not a new pattern every frame) and gentle handheld drift.
