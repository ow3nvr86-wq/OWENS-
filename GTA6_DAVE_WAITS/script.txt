This man has been waiting for one video game... for thirteen years.
September twenty thirteen. GTA 5 comes out. Dave plays it all night. That same night, his son is born.
Then Rockstar puts GTA 5 out again, on new consoles. Then again, on newer ones. Dave buys it. Three times.
Twenty twenty-three. The first GTA 6 trailer drops. Dave cries. Ninety million views in one day.
Release date: twenty twenty-five. Delayed. May twenty twenty-six. Delayed again. Dave's beard goes grey.
Finally, November nineteenth, it's here. And his son, born the same night as GTA 5? Still too young to play it.
