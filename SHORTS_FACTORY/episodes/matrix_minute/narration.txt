Everything he ever saw was a computer program. And he chose to find out.

Thomas Anderson writes code by day and hacks by night, as Neo. A stranger named Morpheus offers him two pills. Blue: go back to his normal life. Red: learn the truth. He takes the red.

He wakes in a pod of goo, one of countless humans the machines farm for energy, while their minds live in a fake world: the Matrix.

On Morpheus's ship, Neo learns kung fu by having it uploaded into his brain. Morpheus believes he's the One. But the Oracle hints he isn't.

Then crewman Cypher sells them out to the Agents, for a comfortable life back inside. Morpheus is captured, so Neo and Trinity storm the building to save him.

Agent Smith shoots Neo dead. But Trinity tells him she loves him, and he wakes up. Now he sees the Matrix as code, stops bullets in mid-air, and destroys Smith from the inside.

Would you take the red pill?
