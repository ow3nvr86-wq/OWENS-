MOVIES IN A MINUTE: THE MATRIX (1999, written and directed by the Wachowskis). Plot retold in our own words.
Claim -> sources
Everything he saw was a computer program (simulated reality) -> Wikipedia ("a simulated reality"); Filmsite ("false reality transmitted into their brain"); Shmoop ("merely... a computer program")
Thomas Anderson, programmer, hacker alias Neo -> Wikipedia ("a computer programmer known as 'Neo' in hacking circles"); Filmsite; Shmoop ("hacker parading as a software programmer")
Morpheus offers red pill (truth) or blue pill (return to normal life); Neo takes red -> Wikipedia; Filmsite; Shmoop
Wakes in a liquid-filled pod; machines harvest humans for energy -> Wikipedia ("harvesting their bioelectric energy"); Filmsite ("liquid-filled pod"); Shmoop ("farm humans for energy")
Minds kept in the Matrix -> Wikipedia ("To pacify their captives, they built the Matrix"); Filmsite
Morpheus's ship (the Nebuchadnezzar, not named in narration) -> Wikipedia; Filmsite
Martial arts/kung fu learned by uploading programs into his brain -> Filmsite ("martial arts via Tank's simulation downloads"); Shmoop (kung fu). Narration does not give the time it took.
Morpheus believes Neo is the One; the Oracle implies he isn't -> Wikipedia ("She implies that Neo is not the One"); Filmsite; Shmoop
Cypher betrays the crew to the Agents in exchange for a comfortable life back in the Matrix -> Wikipedia (betrayal); Filmsite ("in exchange for a reinserted, blissful and comfortable life in the Matrix"); Shmoop ("so he can get back into the Matrix")
Morpheus captured; Neo and Trinity go back in and free him -> Wikipedia; Filmsite; Shmoop
Agent Smith shoots and kills Neo -> Wikipedia ("kills Neo"); Filmsite ("emptied his revolver into Neo's body")
Trinity confesses her love; Neo revives -> Wikipedia; Filmsite ("I love you... he revived")
He perceives the Matrix as code, stops bullets in mid-air, destroys Smith from the inside -> Wikipedia ("perceive and manipulate its code", "destroys Smith"); Filmsite ("lines of downward-streaming computer code", bullets stopped "in mid-air", light from inside Smith); Shmoop ("go inside... and blow them up")
Cut / not stated: the year (Morpheus himself is unsure), Cypher's wish to forget (not in sources checked), Tank killing Cypher, Sentinels/EMP and the final phone call (left out for time).
Rights: original animation; stylized characters, no actor likenesses; no film footage, audio, logos or music; quotes paraphrased; synthesized score; cartoon-style violence without gore. Green "code rain" shown as a generic falling-glyph effect, not the film's design.
