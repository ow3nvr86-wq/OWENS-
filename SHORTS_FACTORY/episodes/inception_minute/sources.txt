MOVIES IN A MINUTE: INCEPTION (2010, written and directed by Christopher Nolan). Plot retold in our own words.
Claim -> sources
2010 film by Christopher Nolan -> Wikipedia ("written and directed by Christopher Nolan", released July 2010); web search summaries (2010 science fiction thriller written and directed by Nolan)
Cobb steals information from people's subconscious while they dream -> Wikipedia ("steals information by infiltrating his targets' subconscious"); Shmoop ("trying to extract information from Saito")
Saito offers Cobb a way home to his children if he performs an inception -> Wikipedia ("clear Cobb's criminal status, allowing him to return home to his children"); GradeSaver ("reunite Cobb with his children in the United States"); Shmoop ("can help him return if Cobb pulls off an 'inception'"). Narration does not mention the criminal charges (one source only).
Target Robert Fischer; idea: dissolve his father's company -> Wikipedia ("the idea to dissolve his father's conglomerate"); Shmoop ("the son will dissolve the father's company"); GradeSaver (Robert Fischer, his father dies)
Team: Ariadne architect, Eames forger, Yusuf chemist, Arthur partner -> Wikipedia; GradeSaver ("Yusuf, a Kenyan chemist", Arthur "Cobb's partner"); Shmoop (Ariadne "an architect", Eames "a forger")
Dream within a dream: first level with Yusuf's van, a hotel, a snowy fortress -> Wikipedia (raining city dreamt by Yusuf, hotel dreamed by Arthur, alpine fortress dreamt by Eames); GradeSaver (falling van in the first layer, hotel lobby, snow-blanketed fortress); Shmoop (fortress in a snow-scape)
The sedative means dying sends you into limbo -> Wikipedia (Yusuf's sedatives send them into "Limbo"); GradeSaver ("powerful sedative may leave whomever is killed in the dream in 'limbo'")
Mal is Cobb's dead wife; he planted the idea her world was not real, and she killed herself over it (still believing she was dreaming) -> Wikipedia ("accidentally incepting in her the belief that the real world was still a dream"; she "still believed she was dreaming"); GradeSaver ("dead ex-wife Mal", "committed suicide")
Mal's projection keeps sabotaging the mission and shoots Fischer -> Wikipedia ("kills Robert"); Shmoop ("shoots and kills Fischer"); GradeSaver ("shot dead")
Cobb and Ariadne go into limbo and bring Fischer back -> Wikipedia ("lost in Limbo", rescued, woken "with a kick"); GradeSaver (Cobb and Ariadne descend; Ariadne wakes Robert)
Fischer's dying father tells him to live his own life; the inception is complete -> Wikipedia ("his dying father telling him to create something for himself"); GradeSaver ("tells him to live his own life, rendering the inception complete")
Saito dies, ends up in limbo; Cobb finds him aged -> Shmoop ("died and ended up in Limbo"); Wikipedia ("finds an aged Saito"); GradeSaver ("Saito, now old")
They wake on the plane; Saito makes a call; Cobb gets through immigration and sees his children -> Wikipedia; Shmoop ("Saito makes a call", "through immigration")
Cobb spins his top and goes to his children before seeing the result -> Wikipedia ("reunites with his children before he can observe the result"); GradeSaver ("while his totem continues to spin"). The film does not show whether it falls; the narration only asks.
Cut / not stated: the criminal charges, the time ratio between levels (sources word it differently), the top originally belonging to Mal (not in sources checked), the folding city, zero-gravity fight and kick details (left out for time; the falling van and floating hotel are shown visually, per GradeSaver).
