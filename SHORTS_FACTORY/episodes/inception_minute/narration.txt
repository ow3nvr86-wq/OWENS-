He can walk into your dreams and steal your secrets. This time, he has to leave one behind.

Dom Cobb steals ideas from people's minds while they dream. A client, Saito, offers him a way home to his kids if he plants one instead: make Robert Fischer break up his dying father's company.

Cobb's team: an architect, Ariadne, plus a forger and a chemist.

They go three dreams deep: a van chase, a hotel, a snowy fortress. With the sedative, dying down there means getting lost in limbo.

And Cobb's dead wife, Mal, keeps showing up. He once planted the idea that her world wasn't real, and she killed herself over it.

Mal shoots Fischer. Cobb and Ariadne dive into limbo to bring him back, and his dying father tells him to live his own life. Idea planted.

Saito dies too. Cobb finds him in limbo, old. They wake on the plane, Saito makes one call, and Cobb goes home.

He spins his top, and doesn't wait to see if it falls.

Did it fall?
