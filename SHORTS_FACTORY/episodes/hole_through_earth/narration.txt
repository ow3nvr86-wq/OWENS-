Jump in here. You come out the other side of the planet in 42 minutes.
Picture a tunnel straight through the Earth. No air, no heat. You step off the edge.
You fall faster and faster, and the whole way down, you feel weightless.
In under a minute, you pass the deepest hole humans ever dug. Twelve kilometers. Not even a tenth of a percent of the way.
About twenty minutes in, you shoot through the center, about as hot as the surface of the Sun, at nearly eight kilometers a second.
Then gravity flips. It pulls you back, slowing you down, until you rise out the far side and stop, right at the top.
Forty-two minutes. Really closer to thirty-eight, because Earth's core is so dense.
Jump in from most of the US, and you'd pop out in the Indian Ocean.
If nobody grabs you, you fall back in. Again and again. Forever.
And if there's air in the tunnel? You'd slow down, and end up stuck, floating at the center of the Earth.
Would you jump?
