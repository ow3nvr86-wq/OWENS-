FICTION. Part 2 of "The Last Town on Earth" (episodes/whittier_town). The town, the Tower, the Whiteout and the count are invented.
Continues from Part 1's ending: the count was 271 and somebody moved into the empty apartment next to yours (9-14).
Part 2: you open the vacant apartment and find a copy of your own, with a jumpsuit numbered 9-14 on the bed. The curfew
seals the doors; your own door is locked from the inside, and through its little window someone wearing your beanie smiles
back. The shutters slam, the lights die, the Whiteout gets in. Morning: the Tower counts 270, everyone is home... and you
are standing outside in the snow, looking up at your own window, where the other you is standing.
