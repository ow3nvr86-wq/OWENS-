Never open the empty apartment next to yours.
Two nights ago, the Tower counted two hundred seventy-one. Tonight, you knock.
Nobody answers. But the door isn't locked.
Inside... it's your apartment. Same lamp. Same bed. Same clock, stopped at ten fifteen.
And on the bed, a jumpsuit. Folded. Number nine-fourteen.
Your number.
Then the speakers crackle: "Doors sealing. Return to your floor."
You run back to your door. It's locked. From the inside.
Through the little window, someone's standing in your apartment.
Wearing your beanie. Smiling.
The shutters slam. The lights die. And the cold gets in.
Six a.m. The Tower counts.
Two hundred seventy.
Everyone's home.
So why are you standing in the snow... looking up at your own window?
