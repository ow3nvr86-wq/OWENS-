He spent nineteen years in prison for a murder he didn't commit. Then one morning, his cell was empty.
1947. Banker Andy Dufresne gets two life sentences for killing his wife and her lover. He's innocent.
In Shawshank, he befriends Red, who can smuggle in anything. Andy asks for a tiny rock hammer, and later, a movie-star poster.
Years pass. He does the guards' taxes, runs the prison library, and launders money for the crooked warden.
Then a new inmate knows who really did it. The warden has him killed.
Andy tells Red about a beach in Mexico. Next morning, roll call. Andy's gone.
The furious warden throws a rock at the poster, and it rips straight through. Behind it: a tunnel.
Nineteen years of digging. He crawls out through a sewer pipe, takes the warden's dirty money, and mails his secret books to the newspaper.
Red gets out after forty years, follows Andy's clues, and finds him on that beach.
Would you have kept digging?
