This entire town lives in one building.
You just moved to Whittier, Alaska. Population: about two hundred seventy. And almost all of them live in the same fourteen-story tower.
Need stamps? The post office is downstairs. Groceries? Downstairs. The police station, the clinic, the church, the laundromat... all inside your building.
Your kids don't even go outside for school. They walk there through a tunnel.
The Army built this tower in the 1950s. Right next to it sits the Buckner Building, the old "city under one roof." Abandoned. Falling apart.
Want to leave? There's only one road out. A single-lane tunnel, two and a half miles through a mountain... and you share it with trains.
Cars and trains take turns. And at night, it closes.
Miss the last one?
You're stuck here till morning.
With everyone you know.
Who all live upstairs.
