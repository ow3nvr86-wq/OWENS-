To save humanity, he had to leave his daughter. He promised he'd come back.

Earth is dying: dust storms, failing crops. Cooper, a farmer and ex-NASA pilot, finds a code in the dust in his daughter Murph's room. She calls it a ghost.

It leads to a secret NASA base, and a wormhole near Saturn. Murph begs him to stay. Cooper leaves her his watch and goes.

Miller's planet: one hour there is seven years on Earth. A giant wave kills Doyle, and twenty-three years are gone.

Then Dr. Mann. He faked his data to get rescued, and tries to kill Cooper.

Low on fuel, Cooper detaches and falls into the black hole, Gargantua, so Brand can make it.

Inside, he sees Murph's bedroom across all of time. He was the ghost. He ticks the data into her watch in Morse code. Murph cracks gravity, and humanity leaves Earth.

Cooper wakes on a station by Saturn. Murph is old now, and sends him to find Brand.

Would you have stayed?
