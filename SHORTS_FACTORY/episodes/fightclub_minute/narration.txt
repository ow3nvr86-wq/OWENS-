His best friend started Fight Club. Problem is, his best friend doesn't exist.
A lonely office worker can't sleep. So he fakes his way into support groups for diseases he doesn't have, and finally sleeps. Until Marla shows up, another faker, and ruins it.
On a business flight, he meets Tyler Durden, a soap salesman. Then his apartment explodes. He calls Tyler. At a bar, Tyler asks him to hit him as hard as he can.
They fight. Soon it's a secret club in a bar basement. Rule one: you don't talk about it.
They move into a crumbling house, and Tyler makes soap from stolen liposuction fat. Then the club becomes an army: Project Mayhem.
When the police kill his friend Bob, he tries to shut it all down, and discovers the truth. He is Tyler.
To stop him, he shoots himself through the cheek. Tyler's gone. But the bombs still go off, and he holds Marla's hand as the credit card towers fall.
Would you have figured it out?
