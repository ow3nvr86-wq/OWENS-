## Changed or hedged for accuracy

- **No suspect appears in the video.** The brief asks who the Zodiac was "believed to be". The honest answer is that no one has been identified. The one person police publicly named was never charged, and the physical evidence did not link him to the crimes (source 7). Later claims by private groups were rejected. The video names no one, and the "suspect" folder props have no names on them.
- **The 37 victims claim is left out.** In 1974 the writer claimed 37 victims, but only five killings and two attempted killings are confirmed. The 60-second script uses only the confirmed figures.
- **"Within a week"** for the 408 solve, because sources date it to 5 or 8 August 1969.
- **"In 1968 and '69"** rather than exact dates, because the attacks ran from December 1968 to October 1969.
- **"From someone calling himself the Zodiac"**: the very first letters were not signed with the name; it appeared days later.

## What is illustrative (not literal)

- **The cipher symbols.** The video's glyphs are generated by code in the style of the Zodiac ciphers (letters, reversed letters, shapes, part-filled circles). They are **not** a transcription of the real ciphers, and this is labelled on screen. The grid sizes are real: the 340 cipher is 17 columns × 20 rows, and the 408 cipher was sent as three parts of 17 × 8.
- **The letters, envelopes, newspapers and handwriting** are recreations drawn by code with a hand-printed font. The only real wording used is "Dear Editor / This is the Zodiac speaking", the 408 phrase "I WILL NOT GIVE YOU MY NAME" and the 340's first decoded line. The newspaper masthead is generic: no real newspaper's front page is reproduced.
- **San Francisco** is a procedural city in the style of the late 1960s: hills, row houses with bay windows, streetlights, period cars. It is not a map of real streets. The police car is a generic 1960s black-and-white sedan, not a specific department's livery.
- **The figure in the street** is a symbolic silhouette with no identifiable features. It is not based on any police sketch or witness description.
- **The investigation room, the investigators and the board** are a recreation. The investigators are faceless mannequins and represent no real detective.
- **The map of attack sites** marks the four confirmed sites at their approximate latitude and longitude. The photographs on the board are drawn by code and are not real crime-scene or location photos. Nothing graphic is shown.

## Facts researched but not used (to keep the Short at 60 s)

- Two other ciphers (the 13-symbol "My name is..." cipher of April 1970 and the 32-symbol cipher with a Bay Area map, June 1970) remain unsolved (1, 2).
- The writer threatened school children in letters of October and November 1969 (1).
- The last letter confirmed as authentic, January 1974, claimed "Me = 37, SFPD = 0" (1).
- The 340 cipher's solution needed a transposition (reading the symbols in a diagonal order) before the substitution could be solved; the team tested about 650,000 variants (3).
- Investigators in Vallejo tried genetic genealogy in 2018 without a definitive result (1, 6).
