WHAT IF GRAVITY TURNED OFF FOR 10 SECONDS?
A 58.3-second vertical science Short, made entirely with code
==============================================================

1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 58.3 s, H.264 + AAC. A woman lifts her coffee on a city street; gravity switches off, her
hand stops - and the coffee keeps rising out of the cup. For ten seconds nothing is pulled down: a basketball flies
dead straight, a fountain keeps rising, a dog floats beside its owner, a man drifts off his sofa. Earth keeps spinning,
so the ground turns away beneath everything and the whole city slowly lifts; cars lose their grip, an airliner climbs.
A countdown: 10 ... 1. "But the floating was never the problem. The landing is." Gravity returns, and everything comes
down at once. The last shot returns to the cup ("So... hold on to your coffee."), so the Short loops.

Everything you see and hear is generated by code:
- Visuals: Blender's Python API (bpy) builds and renders every frame: a city street (cafe, shops, bus stop, parked and
  moving cars, a bus, a plaza with a fountain, a park with a basketball court), an apartment, Earth in space, an
  airliner, 14 stylised characters and a dog. No stock footage, downloaded models, textures or templates.
- Physics: a small model of the scenario inside animation.py (free motion, the drift caused by Earth's spin, gravity
  returning with landings, bounces and sliding; ~20,000 fountain droplets; litter; car suspension).
- Printed props: tools/textures.py (Pillow) draws shop signs, the cup sleeve, a newspaper, the window view, wall art.
- Captions: tools/captions.py (Pillow) draws the word-by-word captions.
- Music and sound: numpy synthesis in animation.py --stage audio. No samples or recordings.
- Narration: a synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M, voice "bm_george"), made by tools/voiceover.py.


FILES
-----
  script.txt          the narration with measured start/end times (145 words)
  hooks.txt           three opening-hook options, how they scored, why option 1 was chosen, the open loop
  storyboard.txt      scene-by-scene storyboard: visuals, characters, camera, lighting, sound, narration, on-screen
                      text, events, transitions (generated from scene_timing.json by tools/voiceover.py)
  animation.py        the whole production in one Blender script: sets, characters, physics, animation, cameras,
                      lighting, render, audio, edit (titles, countdown, captions) and encode; objects are organised
                      in named collections (City, Chars, Water, Room, Space, FX, Rig, Lights)
  subtitles.srt       subtitles timed from the synthesised voice (short phrases, max 3 words)
  sound_design.txt    every music and sound cue with its time, plus a music guide for re-scoring
  sources.txt         sources, claim table, the one calculation, confirmed vs estimated vs speculation
  README.txt          this file
  final_video.mp4     the finished Short (narration, captions, music and sound)

  scene_timing.json   the single source of truth for timing (scenes, shots, slow-motion clocks, narration, titles)
  render_all.sh       resumable full render in 200-frame chunks
  tools/              voiceover.py, captions.py, textures.py, contact.py (look-dev contact sheets)
  audio/              narration.wav, mix_with_voice.wav (final mix), mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only)
  renders/            preview.mp4 (low-resolution preview), final_video_no_voice.mp4 (no narration or captions,
                      for recording your own voice)
  assets/fonts/       Anton, Old Standard TT, Covered By Your Grace (SIL OFL)


THE SIX SCENES (times follow the measured voice)
------------------------------------------------
  0.0-8.3   THE FIRST MOMENT          The coffee rises out of the cup (GRAVITY: OFF); a fast tracking shot down the
                                      street - feet leave the pavement, a hat drifts off, parked cars lift.
  8.3-17.4  EVERYTHING STARTS FLOATING The ball flies straight over the hoop and the jumper grabs the rim; the fountain
                                      keeps rising; a dog floats beside its owner; a man drifts up off his sofa.
  17.4-31.0 THE WORLD BECOMES CHAOTIC Earth from space (1,670 km/h at the equator); the straight-line illustration; a
                                      car floating on with its wheels spinning; an airliner climbing over the street.
  31.0-42.1 THE 10 SECONDS RUN OUT    The countdown 10 ... 1: an aerial shot, a cafe table rising past a height scale,
                                      the woman floating high above the pavement, the floating city seen from below.
  42.1-50.0 GRAVITY RETURNS           GRAVITY: RESTORED - everything drops at once (slow motion): cars slam onto the
                                      road and bounce, the fountain collapses in a downpour, people land in a crouch.
  50.0-58.3 THE FINAL PAYOFF          The quiet, scattered street; the woman holding her cup in both hands.


HOW TO RUN IT
-------------
Requirements: Blender 4.2 LTS (tested with 4.2.3), ffmpeg, Python 3 with Pillow and numpy (for tools/textures.py and
tools/captions.py). The voice step needs Python with kokoro-onnx and soundfile, plus kokoro-v1.0.onnx and
voices-v1.0.bin from the kokoro-onnx releases. In this repository's Claude Code cloud sessions, the SessionStart hook
installs all of it and exports $BLENDER, $SHORTS_PY, $KOKORO_PY and $KOKORO_DIR.

  cd WHAT_IF_GRAVITY_TURNED_OFF
  $SHORTS_PY tools/textures.py                      # printed props -> build/tex/ (animation.py also runs it if missing)
  $KOKORO_PY tools/voiceover.py                     # narration.wav, subtitles.srt, script.txt, storyboard.txt
  $SHORTS_PY tools/captions.py                      # caption images (the edit stage also runs it if missing)
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality preview --stills 60,303,399,900,1245,1461
                                                    # look-dev stills (frame numbers; --out to choose a folder)
  ./render_all.sh preview                           # full 270x480 preview -> renders/preview_frames/
  ./render_all.sh final                             # full 540x960 render  -> renders/frames/ (resumable)
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage audio
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit                  # -> final_video.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --no-voice       # -> renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --out renders/preview_frames --output preview.mp4 --bitrate 3500

On Windows or macOS, replace $BLENDER with the path to blender.exe or Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender and run
render_all.sh's loop by hand (or simply: blender -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality final).

To edit in CapCut instead: render the frames, run the audio stage, then import renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4 (or the
frames plus audio/mix_no_voice.wav), add audio/narration.wav, and use subtitles.srt for the captions.

To retime: edit scene_timing.json (each shot's "tau" keys are its slow-motion clock), re-run tools/voiceover.py and
tools/captions.py, then render, audio and edit. Re-render only the shots whose 3D content or timing you changed.

RENDER TIMES AND FINAL FILES
----------------------------
Measured on this machine (4 CPU cores, no GPU; Cycles with OIDN denoising):
  final   540x960, 6 samples, upscaled 2x in the edit   7.1 s per frame (+ scene load) -> 3 h 42 min for 1,749 frames
                                                        (run as two background jobs; render_all.sh resumes where it stopped)
  preview 270x480, 4 samples                            1.6 s per frame -> 58 minutes
  look-dev stills: about 2-3 minutes per contact sheet of 21 preview stills
  audio stage about 20 s; edit stage about 4.5 minutes per video. A desktop GPU renders all of this many times faster.

Final files (checked with ffprobe and ffmpeg ebur128):
  final_video.mp4                    1080x1920, 30 fps, 58.3 s, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo, 57.8 MB, -13.9 LUFS,
                                     true peak -1.9 dBTP
  renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4   same picture without captions or narration (titles and countdown kept),
                                     57.9 MB, -13.9 LUFS, true peak -1.4 dBTP
  renders/preview.mp4                the 270x480 preview, upscaled; 26.8 MB

To record your own voice: import renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4 into an editor (e.g. CapCut) and read script.txt,
using the start times in script.txt or subtitles.srt.


ACCURACY
--------
- The scenario is hypothetical and labelled on screen: "A HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO - SHOWN IN SLOW MOTION"; the globe
  diagram is labelled "ILLUSTRATION - NOT TO SCALE"; the return of gravity is labelled "SLOW MOTION".
- Physics shown: inertia (nothing shoots upward; moving things keep moving), Earth's rotation (the ground turns away
  beneath everything: ~1.7 m in 10 s near the equator, computed from NASA's Earth data), friction needs weight (tyres
  lose grip), lift no longer balanced by weight (the airliner climbs), and falling with the velocity things already had.
- Estimates used only for the look (not stated as numbers): people's push-off speeds, car-spring rebound, the fountain
  staying a clean column, the airliner's climb. Details in sources.txt, section 4.
- Not claimed: anything about the air (the atmosphere does not disappear in the video), injuries (non-graphic).
- All places, shops, brands and characters are invented.
