Northwind Labs releases an open-weights 3B model for the Raspberry Pi 5

Northwind Labs today released Northwind-3B, an open-weights language model with 3 billion parameters, under the Apache 2.0 licence.

The company says a 4-bit quantised version runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of RAM at about 6 tokens per second, with no internet connection required.

Northwind says the model is intended for offline assistants, home automation and education. It does not claim the model matches larger cloud models on reasoning benchmarks.

The weights and an evaluation report are available on the company's model page.
