Battery cell reaches 80 percent charge in five minutes in lab tests

Researchers at a sample university report a lithium-ion cell design that reached 80 percent charge in five minutes under laboratory conditions.

The team tested coin-sized cells, not full vehicle packs, and reports capacity retention of 90 percent after 1,000 cycles.

The authors say commercial use would need further testing at larger sizes and that manufacturing costs are not yet known.
