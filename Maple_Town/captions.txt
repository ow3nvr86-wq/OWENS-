Everyone in Maple Town knew one rule.
Never talk to the kid in the grey hoodie.
He moved into the empty house on Oak Street.
He never spoke.
He just walked the town, every night, alone.
People said he was a hacker.
People said he was cursed.
Then, one night, the bank alarm went off.
Robbers.
They took everything, and blocked the only road out.
Nobody moved.
Except him.
He walked straight up to them, and typed one word.
The robbers froze.
Then they dropped the money, and ran.
Turns out the quiet kid was not a hacker.
He was the owner of the game.
And he had been watching how we treat new players.
