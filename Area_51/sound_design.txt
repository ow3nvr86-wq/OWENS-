10-15 dB below the voice) so no effect ever covers a line.

Files: audio/mix_with_voice.wav (final mix, -14 LUFS), audio/mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only, for a
voice recorded in CapCut), audio/narration.wav (voice only).

BACKGROUND MUSIC RECOMMENDATIONS (if you re-score it in an editor)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Style: dark, minimal mystery underscore: low synth or string drones, a muted pulse, sparse piano; no vocals,
  about 80-95 bpm. Think slow-burn documentary, not sci-fi trailer: avoid theremins and "alien" clichés.
- 0-5 s: one held low note and a three-note piano phrase under the hook.
- 5-17 s: wind-swept drone with radio static; let it swell into the cut to the hangar.
- 17-30 s: a low pulse that builds as the lights come on and the A-12 is revealed; a deep hit on the reveal.
- 30-43 s: thin out; a single high sustained tone under the light in the sky; near silence for the headlines.
- 43-52 s: the pulse returns, slower and steadier, under the declassified files.
- 52-60 s: back to the drone and the piano phrase; one final deep hit as the hangar doors shut; ring out into black.
- Keep the narration 10-15 dB above the music; YouTube Shorts plays back at about -14 LUFS.
- Use only music you have the rights to (your own, a licensed library, or YouTube Audio Library tracks that allow it).
