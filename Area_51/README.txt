AREA 51 - THE BASE THAT DID NOT EXIST
A 60-second vertical documentary Short, made entirely with code
============================================================

1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 60 s, H.264 + AAC. A dark mystery documentary about the secret test site at Groom Lake,
Nevada: why the CIA chose it in 1955, the U-2 and A-12 OXCART spy planes tested there, how their high-altitude
flights fuelled UFO reports, the CIA history that finally named Area 51 in 2013, and what is still classified. It
ends on the question: WHAT ELSE IS OUT THERE?

Everything you see and hear is generated by code:
- Visuals: Blender's Python API (bpy) builds and renders every frame. There is no stock footage, no downloaded
  model or texture, and no animation template.
- Paper props and signs: tools/textures.py (Pillow/numpy) draws the warning signs, front pages, folders, the CIA
  history's cover, documents, a sketch map, the "photographs" (code-drawn plan views) and the stamps.
- Music and sound: numpy synthesis in animation.py --stage audio. There are no samples or recordings.
- Narration: a synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M, voice "bm_george"), made by tools/voiceover.py.


FILES
-----
  script.txt          narration, scene by scene, with start and end times (127 words)
  storyboard.txt      scene-by-scene storyboard and editing instructions: visuals, camera moves, narration,
                      on-screen text, animation events, transitions (generated from scene_timing.json)
  animation.py        the whole production in one Blender script: 3D scene, animation, render, audio, edit, encode
  subtitles.srt       subtitles timed from the synthesised voice
  sound_design.txt    every music and sound cue with its time, plus background-music recommendations
  sources.txt         sources, a claim-by-claim fact table, confirmed vs. disputed vs. speculation, what is illustrative
  README.txt          this file
  final_video.mp4     the finished Short (narration, captions, music and sound)

  scene_timing.json   the single source of truth for timing (shots, narration slots, titles, events)
  render_all.sh       resumable full render in 200-frame chunks
  tools/voiceover.py  narration synthesis; writes script.txt, subtitles.srt, storyboard.txt, build/vo_timing.json
  tools/textures.py   procedural paper props and signs (written to build/tex/)
  tools/contact.py    contact sheets of look-dev stills
  audio/              narration.wav, mix_with_voice.wav (final mix), mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only)
  renders/            preview.mp4 (low-resolution preview), final_video_no_voice.mp4 (no narration or captions, for
                      recording your own voice in CapCut)
  assets/fonts/       Anton, Old Standard TT, Courier Prime, Covered By Your Grace (SIL OFL), Special Elite (Apache 2.0)


THE SIX SCENES
--------------
 0.0-5.0   THE DESERT      Darkness, then first light: the camera glides over a ridge of the Nevada ranges toward a
                           white dry lakebed and the lights of a remote airfield. AREA 51 - NEVADA, USA.
 5.0-17.0  THE BASE        A low flyover of the base (runway, hangars, tower, buildings, fences), then the perimeter
                           road: boundary posts, a fence line, a guard tower, a patrol truck and the RESTRICTED AREA sign.
 17.0-30.0 THE AIRCRAFT    Inside a dark hangar, banks of lights clunk on over a silver U-2 (1955). Then a cold light
                           rakes along a black A-12 OXCART (1962): chines, engine spikes, canted fins.
 30.0-43.0 THE UFO MYSTERY At dusk the U-2 crosses the sky at high altitude until it is a glinting point. At night a
                           light hangs over the ridge while a lone observer watches. Then 1950s-style headlines.
 43.0-51.6 THE FILES       A records archive: the declassified CIA history is stamped DECLASSIFIED; pages, a site map
                           and photographs of the aircraft slide onto the table. DECLASSIFIED - 2013.
 51.6-60.0 STILL HIDING?   The base at night. The camera crosses the apron toward one lit hangar; its doors roll shut
                           until the light is a slit and is gone. THE MYSTERY OF AREA 51. WHAT ELSE IS OUT THERE?


HOW TO RUN IT
-------------
Requirements: Blender 4.2 LTS (tested with 4.2.3), ffmpeg, Python 3 with Pillow and numpy (for tools/textures.py).
The voice step needs Python with kokoro-onnx and soundfile, plus kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin from the
kokoro-onnx releases. In this repository's Claude Code cloud sessions, the SessionStart hook installs all of it and
exports $BLENDER, $SHORTS_PY, $KOKORO_PY and $KOKORO_DIR.

  cd Area_51
  $SHORTS_PY tools/textures.py                      # paper props -> build/tex/ (animation.py also runs it if missing)
  $KOKORO_PY tools/voiceover.py                     # narration.wav, subtitles.srt, script.txt, storyboard.txt
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality preview --stills 60,200,420,600,800,960,1090,1200,1380,1480,1650
                                                    # look-dev stills -> build or renders/frames (use --out to choose)
  ./render_all.sh preview                           # full 270x480 preview -> renders/preview_frames/
  ./render_all.sh final                             # full 540x960 render  -> renders/frames/ (resumable)
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage audio
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit                  # -> final_video.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --no-voice       # -> renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --out renders/preview_frames --output preview.mp4 --bitrate 3500

On Windows or macOS, replace $BLENDER with the path to blender.exe or Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender and run
render_all.sh's loop by hand (or just: blender -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality final).

Render budget on 4 CPU cores with no GPU (Cycles, OIDN denoising):
  final   540x960, 6 samples, upscaled 2x in the edit   roughly 8-20 s per frame, about 5-8 hours for 1,770 frames
  preview 270x480, 4 samples                            roughly 2-6 s per frame, about 1.5-2 hours
  The last second (black with the closing title) is drawn in the edit. A desktop GPU renders this many times faster.

To retime: edit scene_timing.json, re-run tools/voiceover.py, then re-run the audio and edit stages. Re-render the 3D
frames only if you move a shot boundary or change anything in the 3D scene.

To record your own voice in CapCut: import renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4 and read script.txt, using the start times
in script.txt or subtitles.srt.


ACCURACY
--------
- Every date, number and name is checked against at least two sources (sources.txt, section 3).
- Confirmed history (the CIA's choice of Groom Lake in 1955, the U-2 and A-12 test programmes, the 2013 release of the
  CIA history) is stated as fact. UFO sightings are presented as reports. The narration quotes the CIA history's own
  finding that U-2 test flights caused "a tremendous increase" in UFO reports; it does not use the disputed CIA
  estimate that spy planes explain more than half of all reports.
- The video never claims that aliens or alien technology were found at Area 51. On screen: NO EVIDENCE OF ALIEN
  TECHNOLOGY (the finding of the Pentagon's AARO historical review, 2024).
- The light the observer watches is a dramatisation of what witnesses described, deliberately left unexplained.
- Recreations, not records: the base layout and perimeter (a composite), the warning sign, the newspapers (fictional
  mastheads, generic headlines), the documents, map and photographs. The CIA history's cover is recreated with its
  real title, authors and date.
- The aircraft are modelled from published dimensions: U-2A about 15.1 m long with a 24.4 m span (natural-metal
  finish, as flown in 1955); A-12 about 31 m long with a 17 m span (titanium, in its later all-black paint).
