## 2. Concept

**Hook:** *"The pause is the move."* This is the real cue from the **Hesitation and Go** drill.

- **0–2s, hook:** total silence and darkness, then one hard dribble echoes.
- **2–10s, proof:** an athlete works through three real Court Craft drills alone at night,
  cut to the beat, with the app's timer counting down between moves.
- **10–15s, call to action:** the app on a phone, then the logo and "Coming soon".

## 7. Music and sound

- From CapCut's built-in library, search "dark trap" or "cinematic hip hop" for something
  around 140 BPM.
- Keep it silent for 0–2s, with only the dribble sound effect, which should echo.
- Put the beat drop exactly on the cut into shot 2, at 2.0s.
- Add a sound effect hit (a "whoosh" or "impact") on the burst in shot 4.
- Duck the music to about 30% under the voiceover lines.

## 8. CapCut build steps

1. Open CapCut desktop and click **New project**. Set the ratio to **9:16**.
2. Go to **AI media → AI image** and generate the hero frame from section 3. Save it.
3. Go to **AI media → AI video**, choose **Image to video** or **Text to video**, attach the
   hero frame as the reference, and generate shots 1, 2, 4 and 5 using the prompts above.
   Generate two takes of each and keep the best one.
4. Import the screen recordings for shots 3 and 6.
5. Drag everything onto the timeline in shot-list order and trim to the times in the table.
6. Speed-ramp shot 4: **Speed → Curve → Custom**, with a dip during the pause and a spike on
   the burst.
7. Add **Text** layers from section 6. Keep them inside TikTok's safe zone, away from the
   bottom 20% and the right edge.
8. Add **Text to speech** for each voiceover line, then run **Auto captions**.
9. Add the music and sound effects from section 7, and line the beat drop up with 2.0s.
10. Build the end card: a black background, the icon image and a text layer.
11. Click **Export** at 1080p, 30fps, high bitrate. Save as `court-craft-the-pause.mp4`.
