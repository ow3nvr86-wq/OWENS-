The biggest leak in gaming history came from a hotel room... through the TV.
Twenty twenty-two. An eighteen-year-old hacker is on bail. Police take his laptop and move him to a hotel for his own safety.
So he plugs a Fire Stick into the hotel TV, grabs his phone, and breaks into Rockstar. Ninety secret clips of GTA 6 hit the internet.
Rockstar says the clean-up cost five million dollars. A court sends him to a secure hospital, indefinitely.
Then, in twenty twenty-three, even the first trailer leaks. So Rockstar drops it early... ninety million views in a day.
And the game? It finally lands November nineteenth.
