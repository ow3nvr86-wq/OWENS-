THE JFK ASSASSINATION - SOURCES AND FACT CHECK
============================================================

Every statement in the narration and every date, time and name on screen was checked against at least two
of the sources below. Where investigations disagree, the video says which body concluded what, and does not
pick a side. Checked November 2026.

3. ESTABLISHED FACT vs. DISPUTED vs. SPECULATION (how the video handles each)
------------------------------------------------------------
Established (stated as fact): the date, time and place; Kennedy killed and Connally wounded; Oswald arrested
the same afternoon, charged, denied it; Ruby killed Oswald on live TV two days later; the Warren Commission
concluded Oswald acted alone; the HSCA concluded a probable conspiracy; scientists later disputed the acoustic
evidence that conclusion relied on; records have been released over decades, including in 2025 and 2026.

Disputed (attributed, never resolved by the video): whether Oswald acted alone (Warren Commission: yes; HSCA:
probably not, on evidence later disputed; DOJ 1988: no persuasive evidence of conspiracy). The video names
which body said what.

Speculation (not presented): no conspiracy theory is described or endorsed; no person or agency is accused of
involvement. The 2025-2026 releases are not described as proving anything. The closing question "WHAT
REALLY HAPPENED?" reflects the documented, ongoing public debate (Gallup 2023: 65% of Americans believe
others were involved), not a claim that the record is wrong.


4. WHAT IS ILLUSTRATIVE (recreations, not records)
------------------------------------------------------------
- All 3D environments are recreations built in code. The Dallas street is a composite of 1963 downtown
  Main Street, not a specific block; Dealey Plaza's layout is simplified (distances approximate).
- The motorcade's composition is simplified (fewer vehicles and motorcycles than in reality). Occupants are
  faceless mannequins seen at a distance. Nothing of the shooting, injuries or any weapon is shown or heard.
- Oswald appears only on a "portrait-style" card with a silhouette and verified facts - not a likeness,
  labelled "ILLUSTRATIVE CARD - NOT A PHOTOGRAPH".
- Newspaper front pages use fictional, generic mastheads ("THE MORNING EDITION", "THE EVENING EDITION");
  their headlines state facts that were reported that weekend.
- Case folders, the reporter's notepad, location "photographs" (drawings), the acoustic printout (labelled
  "ILLUSTRATIVE RECREATION"), the declassified pages (generic typed lines and redaction bars) and the release
  stamps are props made for the video. The report covers carry the real titles of the two reports.
- The roof-top sign frame on the Depository is shown empty (no lettering).


5. FACTS CHECKED BUT CUT (for time)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Kennedy's itinerary: San Antonio, Houston, Fort Worth, Dallas, then Austin; the Trade Mart luncheon.
- Lyndon B. Johnson sworn in at 2:38 p.m. aboard Air Force One by Judge Sarah T. Hughes (kept on the front
  page and the timeline card only).
- The Zapruder film; the "single bullet" conclusion; the Dictabelt recording itself; the 1992 JFK Records Act
  and the Assassination Records Review Board; the 2017-2023 releases.
- Executive Order 14176 (January 2025) directing full release; some 2026 pages still carry redactions
  (per JFKFACTS) - not needed for the claim made.
- Gallup trend (52% in 1963, 81% in 1976, 61% in 2013, 65% in 2023) - informs the closing line, not stated.
