THE JFK ASSASSINATION - DALLAS, NOVEMBER 22, 1963
A 60-second vertical documentary Short, made entirely with code
============================================================

1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 60 s, H.264 + AAC. A cinematic dark-history documentary: the president's visit to
Dallas, the motorcade, Dealey Plaza, the arrest of Lee Harvey Oswald, his death two days later, the Warren
Commission (1964), the House Select Committee on Assassinations (1979), and the records still being released
(2025, 2026). It ends on the question the record has never settled for the public: WHAT REALLY HAPPENED?

Everything you see and hear is generated by code:
- Visuals: Blender's Python API (bpy) builds and renders every frame. There is no stock footage, no
  downloaded model or texture, and no animation template.
- Paper props: tools/textures.py (Pillow/numpy) draws the front pages, case folders, portrait card, notepad,
  photographs (drawings), timeline cards, plaza diagram, TV bulletin, report covers, documents, stamps, signs
  and flags.
- Music and sound: numpy synthesis in animation.py --stage audio. There are no samples or recordings.
- Narration: a synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M, voice "bm_george"), made by tools/voiceover.py.


FILES
-----
  script.txt          narration, scene by scene, with start and end times (145 words)
  storyboard.txt      scene-by-scene storyboard and editing instructions: visuals, camera moves, narration,
                      on-screen text, animation events, transitions (generated from scene_timing.json)
  animation.py        the whole production in one Blender script: 3D scene, animation, render, audio, edit, encode
  subtitles.srt       subtitles timed from the synthesised voice
  sound_design.txt    every music and sound cue with its time, plus background-music recommendations
  sources.txt         sources, a claim-by-claim fact table, fact vs. disputed vs. speculation, what is illustrative
  README.txt          this file
  final_video.mp4     the finished Short (narration, captions, music and sound)

  scene_timing.json   the single source of truth for timing (shots, narration slots, titles, events)
  render_all.sh       resumable full render in 200-frame chunks
  tools/voiceover.py  narration synthesis; writes script.txt, subtitles.srt, storyboard.txt, build/vo_timing.json
  tools/textures.py   procedural paper props (written to build/tex/)
  tools/contact.py    contact sheets of look-dev stills
  audio/              narration.wav, mix_with_voice.wav (final mix), mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only)
  renders/            preview.mp4 (low-resolution preview), final_video_no_voice.mp4 (no narration or captions, for
                      recording your own voice in CapCut)
  assets/fonts/       Anton, Old Standard TT, Courier Prime, Covered By Your Grace (SIL OFL), Special Elite (Apache 2.0)


THE SIX SCENES
--------------
 0.0-4.6   THE OPENING     Darkness. A 1963 front page, PRESIDENT KENNEDY ASSASSINATED, fades up under one raking
                           light. The camera pushes toward the headline.
 4.6-11.4  DALLAS, 1963    Main Street at midday under breaking cloud: period buildings, shop signs and crowds
                           several deep (some waving). The motorcade comes on: police motorcycles, the white lead
                           car, the open dark-blue limousine with its fender flags, the follow-up cars, a press bus.
 11.4-19.6 THE MOMENT      A wide, distant view of Dealey Plaza: the Texas School Book Depository, the Elm Street
                           curve and the motorcade on it. At "shots rang out" the sound drops away, the colour
                           drains, pigeons scatter from the rooftops and the camera lifts away. Nothing of the
                           shooting is shown. A front page spins in: THE PRESIDENT IS DEAD.
 19.6-36.6 INVESTIGATION   Dallas police headquarters, recreated: desk, lamp, typewriter, case folders, photographs
                           and a portrait-style card for Lee Harvey Oswald (a silhouette and verified facts, no
                           likeness). Then a reporter's notepad ("I'M JUST A PATSY!"). A console TV flickers on with
                           a bulletin, and the camera moves along the timeline board to NOV 24.
 36.6-52.7 THE QUESTIONS   A records archive with steel shelving and light through high windows. The Warren
                           Commission and House Select Committee report covers. An acoustic-analysis printout.
                           Released pages slide in and stack, stamped RELEASED 2025 and RELEASED 2026.
 52.7-60.0 THE FINAL SHOT  Dealey Plaza at night in fog and lamplight. The camera pulls back and up from the
                           Depository: MORE THAN 60 YEARS LATER... then WHAT REALLY HAPPENED? Fade to black.


HOW TO RUN IT
-------------
Requirements: Blender 4.2 LTS (tested with 4.2.3), ffmpeg, Python 3 with Pillow and numpy (for tools/textures.py).
The voice step needs Python with kokoro-onnx and soundfile, plus kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin from the
kokoro-onnx releases. In this repository's Claude Code cloud sessions, the SessionStart hook installs all of it and
exports $BLENDER, $SHORTS_PY, $KOKORO_PY and $KOKORO_DIR.

  cd JFK_Assassination
  $SHORTS_PY tools/textures.py                      # paper props -> build/tex/ (animation.py also runs it if missing)
  $KOKORO_PY tools/voiceover.py                     # narration.wav, subtitles.srt, script.txt, storyboard.txt
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality preview --stills 60,190,400,530,700,1030,1150,1500,1680
                                                    # look-dev stills -> renders/frames (use --out to choose)
  ./render_all.sh preview                           # full 270x480 preview -> renders/preview_frames/
  ./render_all.sh final                             # full 540x960 render  -> renders/frames/ (resumable)
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage audio
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit                  # -> final_video.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --no-voice       # -> renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4
  $BLENDER -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --stage edit --out renders/preview_frames --output preview.mp4 --bitrate 3500

On Windows or macOS, replace $BLENDER with the path to blender.exe or Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender and run
render_all.sh's loop by hand (or just: blender -b --factory-startup -P animation.py -- --quality final).

Render budget on 4 CPU cores with no GPU (Cycles, OIDN denoising):
  final   540x960, 6 samples, upscaled 2x in the edit   about 12-23 s per frame, so about 6-9 hours for 1,770 frames
  preview 270x480, 4 samples                            about 2-5 s per frame, so about 1.5 hours
  The last second (black with the closing title) is drawn in the edit. A desktop GPU renders this many times faster.

To retime: edit scene_timing.json, re-run tools/voiceover.py, then re-run the audio and edit stages. Re-render the 3D
frames only if you move a shot boundary or change anything in the 3D scene.

To record your own voice in CapCut: import renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4 and read script.txt, using the start times
in script.txt or subtitles.srt.


ACCURACY AND RESPECT
--------------------
- Every date, time, name and number is checked against at least two sources (sources.txt, section 2).
- Disputed conclusions are attributed, not resolved. The Warren Commission (1964) said Oswald acted alone. The House
  Select Committee (1979) found a probable conspiracy, based on acoustic evidence that a National Academy of Sciences
  panel later disputed (1982). The video says exactly that and nothing more.
- No conspiracy theory is presented, and no person or agency is accused. The 2025-2026 releases are not described as
  proving anything; experts reported that they did not overturn the established account.
- The shooting, injuries and any weapon are never shown or heard. There are no gunshot sounds. At that moment the video
  cuts to near-silence, pigeons and a drained, distant view.
- The motorcade is seen at a distance. Its occupants are faceless mannequins. The President and Mrs. Kennedy are
  suggested only by their positions in the car and Mrs. Kennedy's pink suit and pillbox hat.
- Lee Harvey Oswald appears only as a portrait-style card: a silhouette and verified facts, labelled "ILLUSTRATIVE
  CARD - NOT A PHOTOGRAPH". He is described as arrested, charged and denying it, in his own words; the conclusions
  about him are attributed to the commissions.
- These are recreations, not records: the city layout (a composite of downtown Main Street and a simplified Dealey
  Plaza), the newspapers (generic, fictional mastheads; factual headlines), folders, notepad, "photographs", acoustic
  printout and declassified pages. The report covers carry the real report titles.
- The light is broken cloud with sun. That matches the record (rain earlier, clear by the motorcade); the brief's
  "cloudy" was kept as clouds, not overcast.
