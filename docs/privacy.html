Privacy Policy

Court Craft. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Court Craft keeps everything on your phone. Your name, your answers, your workouts and your scores never leave the device, are never sent to a server, and are never shared or sold. There are no accounts, no tracking and no advertising.

What the app stores

The app saves the following in its own storage on your device, so it can remember your plan between sessions:

  • the first name you enter during setup
  • your answers to the setup questions
  • your training log, cycle position and completed drills
  • your drill timers and results
  • whether you have unlocked the paid features

This information stays in the app's private storage. Nobody else can read it, including the developer. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account for you.
  • It does not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • It does not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Payments

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, the payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Court Craft never sees or stores your card details, billing address or Apple Account information. Apple provides the developer with anonymous sales totals only.

Children

Court Craft does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because it does not transmit information off the device at all. If you are under 18, please go through your training plan with a parent, guardian or coach first.

Your rights

Because your information never leaves your phone, you control all of it directly. To erase everything, delete the app. There is no server-side copy to request, correct or delete.
