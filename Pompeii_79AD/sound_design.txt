============================================================

usable. The narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M, voice bm_george) made by tools/voiceover.py; music
and ambience duck under it (about 10-15 dB below the voice) so no effect covers a line.

Files: audio/mix_with_voice.wav (final mix, -14 LUFS), audio/mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only, for a
voice recorded in CapCut), audio/narration.wav (voice only).
