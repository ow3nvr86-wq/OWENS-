WHAT IF YOU WERE THE ONLY MAN ALIVE? PART 2 - OPENING HOOKS AND ENDING
=====================================================================

The hook was rebuilt after the first delivery. What was wrong with the first version (kept here so it isn't repeated):
- Frame 1 was a dim tracking shot of a man walking; nothing happened for 1.6 s, and the payoff (his face on the giant
  screen) only arrived at 2.0 s.
- The first words - "You're the only man alive..." - were recap of Part 1. The new information ("the entire world is
  looking for you") only arrived at about 2.5 s.
- "PART 2" was a tiny tag that nobody would read; with the sound off, the first second said nothing.
- The sound opened on a quiet drone; the first hit came at 2.0 s.

Three hooks for the rebuilt opening. Scores are this production's own (25 = best), weighing the first second above all:
does frame 1 stop the scroll, do the first words say something new, and does it work with the sound off.

HOOK A  (picture + number)                                                                          (SELECTED)
        Frame 1: across an empty street a building-sized screen glitches on with HIS FACE and THE ENTIRE WORLD IS
        SEARCHING FOR YOU; he is a tiny figure standing alone in the road beneath it. PART 2 in large amber type is on
        screen from frame 1. A hard hit on frame 1; a riser; a fast push into his face on the screen at 1.4 s; a
        close-up of him staring up at 2.5 s.
        Words (from 0.12 s): "4 billion people are searching for one man... you."
        Why it wins: the payoff image is the first frame, the first word is a number, the twist word lands last, and
        it reads completely with the sound off (title, on-screen text, his face, the caption "4 BILLION PEOPLE").
                                                                                                      Score 24/25
HOOK B  (picture + statement)  Same picture. "Every screen on Earth just switched to your face."
        Strong and visual, but it describes what we can already see; it adds no stakes.            Score 21/25
HOOK C  (the original)  A walking shot, the screen switching on at 1.6 s. "You're the only man alive... and now the
        entire world is looking for you."
        Recap first, payoff late.                                                                   Score 15/25

RETENTION
- 0.0 s: the screen is already on with his face (glitching), PART 2 on screen, impact. 0.12 s: "4 BILLION PEOPLE".
- 1.4 s: cut - a fast push into his face on the screen. 2.5 s: cut - the close-up of him looking up; THE WORLD IS
  SEARCHING. lands as the line ends.
- Fast cuts through the montage (4.2-10.4 s): presenter, giant screens, a sea of people, a situation room, a lab -
  a new shot every 0.5-2.3 s, with stings, static, glitches and a storm of phone notifications.
- Scale goes up scene by scene so Part 2 feels bigger than Part 1: a street, then a square of thousands, then an
  enormous research facility with a holographic globe, then a timeline of the whole planet.
- The pulse stops dead for the black government car; the facility is slow, vast and cold; the timeline ticks; the
  lights go down; the symbol appears; a glitch cuts everything.

ENDING (the cliffhanger)
"You're the only person who might know why everyone else disappeared." The scientist turns to him, the lights dim, an
unknown symbol fills the wall, he stares at it with no idea what it means - the picture glitches and breaks up, and
PART 3? sits on a near-black frame. Nothing is answered: the symbol is never explained.
