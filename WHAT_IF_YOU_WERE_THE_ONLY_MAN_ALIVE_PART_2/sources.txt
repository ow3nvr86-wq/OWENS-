2. CHECKED BUT NOT USED
-----------------------
- "8 billion people are searching for you" was considered for the hook and rejected: 8.2 billion is the world's whole
  population, but in the story every man (about half of it) has gone, so only about 4 billion are left to search.


3. WHAT IS INVENTED (and made with code)
----------------------------------------
- The main character (the same procedural figure as Part 1, built by build_human with the same specification), the
  scientists, the facility director, the news presenter, the officials, the facility staff and the crowds: all
  procedural 3D people made by animation.py. Nobody real is depicted or imitated.
- The city street, the giant screen, the government car, the square, the newsroom, the situation room, the lab, the
  research facility and every screen graphic (tools/textures.py), including the channel GLOBAL 24.
