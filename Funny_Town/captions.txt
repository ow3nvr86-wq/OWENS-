Everyone in Maple Town was tired of boring mayors.
So, as a joke, they voted for a dog.
His name was Biscuit.
His first law: nobody is allowed to be sad.
His second law: the mailman is banned.
Obviously.
Taxes are now paid in snacks.
Then, a robber tried to rob the bank.
Biscuit walked up, sat down, and stared at him.
The robber gave back everything, and begged for a belly rub.
Crime dropped to zero. Happiness doubled.
Best mayor the town ever had.
Until a cat entered the next election.
