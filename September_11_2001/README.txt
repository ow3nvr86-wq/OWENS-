SEPTEMBER 11, 2001: THE DAY THE WORLD STOOD STILL
A 30-second vertical documentary Short in modern 2D motion graphics, made entirely with code
=============================================================================================

1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 30.0 s, H.264 + AAC, -14 LUFS. For the Hidden Archives channel.

2D ONLY: no Blender, no 3D. Every frame is drawn with pycairo (Python) as clean vector shapes with gradients and
soft light; numpy adds the grade, bloom, vignette and grain; Pillow sets the type (Inter, Anton); FFmpeg encodes.
(The brief suggested Pillow or Pygame; pycairo gives anti-aliased vector drawing at any zoom. Pillow is used for
all typography.)

FILES
  final_video.mp4                    the finished Short
  renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4   music and sound only, no narration or captions
  renders/preview.mp4                the half-resolution preview rendered and checked before the final
  script.txt                         the narration with measured times and the three opening hooks (A chosen)
  storyboard.txt                     scene by scene: visuals, motion, text, narration, sound, transitions
  sources.txt                        sources, the facts used, a claim-by-claim check, what is reconstruction
  sound_design.txt                   every sound cue with its time (generated by audio.py)
  subtitles.srt, scene_timing.json   subtitles; scenes, narration, titles and events

  render.py        compositor: edit timeline, transitions (slide, zoom, dissolve), finish, captions, encode
  audio.py         synthesised ambience, pads, piano, tones; ducking under the voice; loudness
  src/gfx.py       the modern 2D drawing library: sky, clouds, river, ferry, skyline, the Twin Towers (to scale),
                   One WTC, smoke and dust, the map, markers, the Pentagon icon, fire engines, silhouettes with rim
                   light, the memorial pool with falling water, the Tribute in Light, the Hidden Archives mark
  src/scenes.py    the scenes;  src/post.py: grade, bloom, vignette, grain;  src/text.py: titles and captions
  src/core.py, figures.py, poses.py, chars.py   camera, easing, the character rig (drawn as silhouettes)
  tools/voiceover.py   narration (Kokoro bm_george), subtitles, script;  tools/contact.py: look-dev sheets
  tools/export_assets.py   writes the illustration sheets in assets/
  assets/          fonts (Inter and Anton, SIL OFL) and exported illustration sheets

HOW TO RUN
  cd September_11_2001
  $KOKORO_PY tools/voiceover.py
  $SHORTS_PY audio.py
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --stills 1.5,6,13.5,20,24,27
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --preview          # about 1 minute
  $SHORTS_PY render.py                    # about 4 minutes on 4 cores
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --no-voice
  $SHORTS_PY tools/export_assets.py
Requirements: Python 3 with numpy, scipy, pillow, pycairo; ffmpeg; kokoro-onnx for the voice; the Inter font.

RESPECT AND ACCURACY
- Facts from the 9/11 Memorial & Museum, the National Park Service (Flight 93), the 9/11 Commission Report and
  the Pentagon Memorial; see sources.txt.
- No aircraft, impact, fire, falling person or injury is shown, and no impact sounds are used. The attackers are
  not named or shown. No dialogue is invented. People are anonymous silhouettes.
- "Nearly 3,000" is said aloud; the exact figure, 2,977, is on screen.
