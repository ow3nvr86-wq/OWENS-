2. VERIFIED FACTS USED
----------------------
- 19 al-Qaeda terrorists hijacked four commercial aircraft. [COMM, MEM]
- 8:46 a.m.  American Airlines Flight 11 struck the North Tower (1 WTC), floors 93-99. [MEM, COMM]
- 9:03 a.m.  United Airlines Flight 175 struck the South Tower (2 WTC), floors 77-85. [MEM, COMM]
- 9:37 a.m.  American Airlines Flight 77 struck the Pentagon. [PENT, COMM]
- 10:03 a.m. United Airlines Flight 93 crashed in a field near Shanksville, Pennsylvania, after passengers and crew
             fought back against the hijackers. [NPS, COMM]
- 9:59 a.m.  the South Tower collapsed; 10:28 a.m. the North Tower collapsed. [COMM, MEM]
- 2,977 people were killed (the 19 hijackers are not counted). [MEM]
- The 9/11 Memorial's two pools sit in the footprints of the Twin Towers; water falls down their walls; the victims'
  names are inscribed in bronze around them. [MEM]
- The Tribute in Light: two vertical beams of light shown over Lower Manhattan each year on the anniversary. [MEM]


3. CLAIM-BY-CLAIM CHECK
-----------------------
"At 8:46 a.m., an ordinary morning changed forever."  8:46 [MEM, COMM]. On screen: New York City / September 11, 2001.
"Two hijacked passenger planes struck the towers of the World Trade Center."  [MEM, COMM]
    On screen: 8:46 North Tower, 9:03 South Tower; "American 11 and United 175"; the marked bands are the floors
    struck (93-99; 77-85).
"Another hit the Pentagon. A fourth crashed in Pennsylvania, after passengers and crew fought back."  [PENT, NPS, COMM]
    On screen: The Pentagon 9:37 AM; Shanksville, PA 10:03 AM; United Flight 93; "Passengers and crew fought back".
"Both towers fell. Nearly 3,000 people were killed, leaving families and communities devastated."
    [COMM, MEM]. On screen: South Tower 9:59 AM / North Tower 10:28 AM; 2,977 lives lost.
"And the world would never be the same."  A closing reflection, not a factual claim.
    On screen: The 9/11 Memorial, New York; We will never forget.


4. HOW THE EVENTS ARE SHOWN (and what is reconstruction)
--------------------------------------------------------
- No aircraft, impact, fire, falling person or injury is shown. The attacks are told with a clock, a timeline, red
  bands on line drawings of the towers at the floors struck, and thin grey smoke.
- The skyline is a simplified illustration of Lower Manhattan seen across the Hudson; apart from the Twin Towers and
  One World Trade Center, the buildings are generic, not portraits of particular buildings.
- The towers are drawn to their real proportions (about 63 m wide, 415-417 m tall, the North Tower's antenna about
  110 m) with the mechanical floors as bands; the façade is simplified.
- The map is a simplified, schematic map of the eastern United States (coastline, lakes, the Pennsylvania border,
  rivers as dashed lines); it is not a survey.
- The responders and the people at the barrier are anonymous silhouettes; no real person is depicted or named.
- The memorial view is an illustration of one pool, not an exact plan; One World Trade Center (completed 2014) stands
  behind it. The Tribute in Light is the annual memorial installation, shown at night.
- The attackers are not named, shown or described beyond the fact of the hijacking.
- Nothing about who was responsible is speculated on beyond the established facts.


5. FACTS CUT FOR TIME
---------------------
The exact seconds of each crash and collapse; the numbers aboard each flight; the 184 people killed at the Pentagon;
the 40 passengers and crew of Flight 93; the collapse of 7 World Trade Center at 5:20 p.m.; the 343 FDNY firefighters
and other first responders killed; the recovery at Ground Zero.
