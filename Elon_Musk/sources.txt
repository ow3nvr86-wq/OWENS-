2. FACTS USED
-------------
- Born 1971 in Pretoria, South Africa (not stated in the video).
- 1999: co-founded X.com, the online bank; it merged with Confinity in 2000 and became PayPal. [BIZ, TIME]
- 2002: eBay bought PayPal for $1.5 billion in stock (Oct 2002). [BIZ, TIME]
- 2002: founded SpaceX. [BIZ, BUILT]
- 2004: led Tesla's early funding round and became chairman; CEO since 2008. [BIZ, BUILT]
- Dec 2015: the first landing of an orbital-class booster (Falcon 9, Landing Zone 1); boosters have since been reflown
  many times. [NASA, SF]
- Nov 2026: about 11,200 Starlink satellites in orbit ("over 10,000"). [KT, AZ]
- 2022: bought Twitter for about $44 billion; renamed X in 2023. [BIZ, TIME]
- 2023: founded xAI; SpaceX acquired xAI in Feb 2026. [CNBC, XAI]
- 2026: the world's richest person on Forbes' lists (about $839B in March 2026; about $908B in Sept 2026). No
  figure is narrated because the number moves daily. [RICH1, RICH2]
- His politics (including his role in the US government in 2025 and spending in US elections) and his ownership of
  X have drawn strong support and strong criticism. [FORBES, BIZ]
