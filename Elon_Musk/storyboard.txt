ELON MUSK: ONE CONTINUOUS JOURNEY - STORYBOARD (ONE SHOT, NO CUTS)
==================================================================
32.0 s, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Modern flat 2D vector animation: deep navy, black, white, electric blue, restrained red.
The camera never cuts: every hand-over is an object or a light inside the shot.

0.0 - 4.6   OPENING. A low-key illustrated portrait (navy-to-ice duotone, electric-blue rim light). An interface
            ring turns around him; on each word of the hook an icon snaps into the ring: a rocket (0.05 s, inside the
            first second), a car (0.79), a satellite (1.78), an AI chip (2.65). On "one man", lines from all four
            icons converge on him. The camera pushes in. Title: ELON MUSK.
            "Rockets. Electric cars. Satellites. AI. One man is behind them all."
4.6 - 9.3   PAYPAL. The ring unspools: nine streams pour out of it to the right, carrying payment chips, coins and
            cards; the portrait slides away as the camera travels right with them.
            Titles: X.COM 1999 / PAYPAL / SOLD TO EBAY 2002; $1.5B.
            "Elon Musk's big break was PayPal, sold to eBay for $1.5 billion."
9.3 - 14.25 TESLA. The streams straighten into the lane markings of a road; towers rise out of the dark as the
            camera arrives; an electric car drives in and the camera tracks it through the city (four parallax planes,
            street lights flashing past). Title: TESLA / INVESTOR 2004 / CEO SINCE 2008.
            "He bet that fortune on Tesla, and helped make electric cars mainstream."
14.25-15.0  THE HAND-OVER. The camera dives into the car's headlight until its light fills the frame...
15.0 - 17.7 SPACEX. ...and pulls back out of the light, which is now the engine light of a rocket lifting off.
            The camera follows it up; the booster separates, flips, falls back with its legs out and lands on the
            pad with a last burn. Titles: SPACEX / FOUNDED 2002; FIRST ORBITAL BOOSTER LANDING / 2015.
            "At SpaceX, rockets learned to land and fly again."
17.7 - 21.55 SPACE. The camera rises along the second stage's trail; the sky turns black, stars come out, the Earth's
            curve rises below with trains of satellites and their links to the ground. Titles: 10,000+ / STARLINK
            SATELLITES IN ORBIT.  "Over 10,000 Starlink satellites now circle the Earth."
21.55-27.55 AI. The camera pushes into one satellite; its links become the edges of a neural network with pulses
            and data read-outs; then the network splits into two camps, blue and red, drifting apart.
            Titles: xAI / FOUNDED 2023; TWITTER / BOUGHT 2022 FOR $44B / NOW X.
            "Now he's racing into AI, while his takeover of Twitter and his politics make him deeply divisive."
27.55-32.0  ENDING. The network folds onto a globe; orbits carry a satellite, a rocket and a car around it.
            Title: HOW MUCH OF THE FUTURE / CAN ONE PERSON SHAPE?  "So how much of the future can one person shape?"
            Fade to black.
