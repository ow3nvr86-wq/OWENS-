THE BATTLE OF MIDWAY - 38.5 s vertical Short (1080x1920, 30 fps, -14 LUFS), one continuous 2D map shot, narrated by George
(Kokoro bm_george). The animation lives in the WW2_Two_Part engine as part3:
  WW2_Two_Part/src/part3.py (the shot), tools/timing_p3.py, tools/george_vo.py, audio.py (build_part3), part3/ (timing, audio)
  script.txt, sources.txt (this folder): script, fact notes, sources.
Files here: final_video.mp4, renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4 (music and effects only), scene_timing.json, sound_design.txt.
Map: Natural Earth coastlines; positions of the fleets are approximate; the Midway atoll is drawn enlarged so it can be seen.
