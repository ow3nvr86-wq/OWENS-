These are open models (Kokoro-82M Apache-2.0; Chatterbox MIT), NOT the ElevenLabs or TikTok voices themselves -
the "type" column only says which popular style each one is aimed at. Chatterbox adds Resemble AI's inaudible
Perth watermark to its output.

What makes them less robotic:
- Chatterbox is an expressive model (emotion "exaggeration" 0.5-0.75) that phrases like a person; Kokoro only
  supplies each voice's timbre as a 9-11 s reference clip (refs/).
- Each script LINE is read in one go (not sentence by sentence), so intonation carries across short sentences.
- Pauses vary slightly line to line; Kokoro reads get a per-line pace (brisk hook, slower payoff).
- Finishing chain like a voiceover booth: rumble cut, a little warmth and presence, soft de-ess, light
  compression, a very short room, -16 LUFS.
- Every file was transcribed with Whisper and matches the script word for word.

Tools: tools/make_refs.py, tools/chatterbox_read.py, tools/kokoro_natural.py, tools/voice_common.py
