SOUND DESIGN - The War Lost to Birds

Narration: the user's own ElevenLabs recording ("Adam - Radio Announcer"), used unchanged (no edits, no re-timing).
Music and SFX are synthesised in audio.py (numpy) and timed in frames from the picture.

LEVELS (mix.py, measured)
  music   = 50 % of the voice's gated RMS (-6.02 dB)
  SFX     = 50 % of the voice's gated RMS (-6.02 dB), peaks capped at 0.7 x the voice peak (measured 0.65 x)
  No ducking and no compression. finish.sh applies one linear gain to -14 LUFS and a -1 dBFS peak limiter to the
  whole mix.

MUSIC
  A jaunty 116 bpm military march: kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, an oom-pah tuba bass, brass chord stabs and a
  piccolo tune (C major). It sits back a little under the farm set-up (f183-364) and lifts during the weeks-of-shooting
  montage (f938-989). It stops dead on "The emus had won." (f1208): a low hit, then a soft drone, and a two-note
  piccolo tag on the emu's smug blink (f1234-1240).
