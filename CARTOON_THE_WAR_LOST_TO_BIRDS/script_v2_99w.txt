In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds. The birds won.
Farmers in Western Australia were losing their wheat to about twenty thousand hungry emus.
So the army sent a major, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets.
The plan was simple. Herd the birds together, then open fire.
But emus don't do plans. They split up and sprinted off in every direction.
One gun jammed. The army went home, came back, and tried again.
When it was over, the major's own count came to about ten bullets for every bird.
Ten bullets. Per bird.
