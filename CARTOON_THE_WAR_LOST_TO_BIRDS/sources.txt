NARRATOR'S VERDICT (opinion, framed as the story's conclusion)
  "The emus had won." / "The emus are winning."  Britannica and others describe the campaign as a failure / defeat.

ILLUSTRATIVE (shown on screen, not claimed as historical detail)
  - The cartoon people, the farm, the truck design, the map, the camp, the Defence desk and the casings mountain.
  - The letters ("PLEASE SEND MACHINE GUNS!", "PLEASE SEND THE ARMY AGAIN!") are invented props standing in for the
    farmers' real requests; their wording is not a quotation.
  - The notepad shows the major's claimed figures (9,860 rounds, 986 emus); the drawing itself is illustrative.
  - The emu wearing the soldier's hat is a joke.
  - The 'NO' stamp stands in for the government's refusals.
