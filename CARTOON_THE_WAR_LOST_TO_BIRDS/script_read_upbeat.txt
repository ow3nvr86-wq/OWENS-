In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds. The birds won!
About twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat!
So the army sent a major, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets!
The plan: herd them together, then fire!
But emus don't do plans! They scattered!
One gun jammed. The army left, came back, and tried again!
By the major's own count, that's ten bullets. Per bird!
