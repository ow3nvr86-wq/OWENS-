A soldier is chasing emus in a truck with a machine gun on the back. The emus are winning.
Western Australia, nineteen thirty-two. Twenty thousand emus are wrecking the wheat farms, so the farmers, many of them ex-soldiers, ask for machine guns.
The army sends a major, two guns, and ten thousand bullets. The plan: herd the emus toward the guns. They scatter instead. One gun jams. And the truck? Too bumpy to aim.
The army pulls out, gets sent back in, and shoots for weeks.
The major's own tally? About ten bullets per emu. When farmers asked for the army again, the answer was no. The emus had won.
