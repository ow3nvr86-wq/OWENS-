In October, 1962, an American spy plane found Soviet missiles in Cuba, 90 miles from Florida.
On October 22nd, President Kennedy went on television, and announced a naval quarantine around Cuba.
Soviet ships were sailing towards the line.
Beneath the waves, a Soviet submarine carried a nuclear torpedo, and American ships were hunting it.
Its officer, Vasili Arkhipov, is credited with refusing to let it fire.
On October 28th, Khrushchev agreed to remove the missiles, if America promised not to invade Cuba.
In a secret deal, America also agreed to remove its missiles from Turkey.
Many historians call it the closest the world has come to nuclear war.
