WORLD WAR II IN TWO PARTS - SOURCES FOR THE SCRIPT CLAIMS
- 1 Sept 1939, Poland invaded; Britain and France declare war on 3 Sept. (Standard chronology.)
- France fell in about six weeks (10 May to 22 June 1940 armistice): History Hit, "10 Facts About the Fall of France"
  https://www.historyhit.com/facts-about-the-fall-of-france-in-world-war-two/ ; Dunkirk: history.com
  https://history.com/topics/world-war-ii/dunkirk
- Barbarossa, 22 June 1941, over 3 million German troops (3.8 million Axis): Australian War Memorial
  https://awm.gov.au/articles/blog/nazi-soviet-war
- Moscow, Dec 1941: German units within about 12-19 miles (19-30 km) of the city; the Soviet counteroffensive opened
  5 Dec: https://historycrunch.com/battle-of-moscow/ , https://www.thecollector.com/battle-moscow-turning-point-wwii/
- Pearl Harbor, 7 Dec 1941. (Standard chronology.)
- Stalingrad surrender, Feb 1943, about 91,000 prisoners: https://www.worldhistory.org/article/2696/battle-of-stalingrad/
- D-Day, about 156,000 troops on 6 June 1944: https://history.com/articles/d-day
- Germany surrendered 7-8 May 1945; Japan announced surrender 15 Aug, signed 2 Sept 1945. (Standard chronology.)
- Deaths: estimated 70-85 million. These are aggregate estimates (e.g. the Eastern Front article cited above);
  they will be re-checked against a major reference before the final edit.
