THE LOST COLONY OF ROANOKE - SOUND DESIGN TIMELINE
============================================================

Everything is synthesised in audio.py with numpy: no samples, no recordings, no third-party audio.
Music: an original slow air in D minor (3/4) on a synthetic recorder, lute and viol, over drones. It is hopeful at
the landing, thins as food runs short, turns martial for the Armada, stops dead when no one answers, and
returns alone and fading at the end.
Mix: music and ambience are ducked about 7 dB under the narration; both mixes are normalised to -14 LUFS (true peak -1.5 dB).

   11.35 -  17.73  They rebuilt an abandoned fort's houses. But food was short, and clashes with local peoples turned deadly.
   18.35 -  24.17  Governor John White sailed to England for supplies... leaving his newborn granddaughter, Virginia Dare.
   25.35 -  31.38  But England was at war with Spain. Ships were held back to fight the Armada. One year became three.
   32.35 -  39.66  August, 1590. White finally returned. At night, his men sounded a trumpet and sang English songs. No one answered.
   40.35 -  47.09  The houses were gone. Carved into a post, one word: CROATOAN. And no cross, their agreed sign of distress.
   48.35 -  54.99  Croatoan was a nearby island, home to allies. Did they go there? Join Native communities? No one has proven it.
   55.30 -  58.99  Archaeologists are still searching... and the forest keeps its secret.
