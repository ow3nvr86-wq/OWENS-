THE LOST COLONY OF ROANOKE - SCENE-BY-SCENE STORYBOARD
=======================================================

59.8 s, 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps. 2D only: every frame is drawn with pycairo vector ink (render.py, src/).
Characters are animated on twos (a new drawing every second frame) with line boil; the camera, sea, fog, moss,
grass, flames and particles move on every frame. Depth comes only from flat layers moved at different speeds (a
multiplane camera). Timing comes from scene_timing.json and the measured narration (build/vo_timing.json).

Palette: charcoal, muted brown, aged parchment, dusty grey, deep burgundy, desaturated green, firelight.
Captions: bold Cinzel capitals in parchment with a dark outline; key words on a burgundy ink plate; at most 3 words
per chunk, 65% of the way down the frame. Titles at the top, clear of the Shorts interface.

SCENE 1 - THE DISAPPEARANCE                                   0.0 - 4.0 s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visual    Dusk. A weathered palisade of great logs in a forest of loblolly pines and live oaks; fog lies on the
          ground; through the entrance, what is left of a house; grey Spanish moss hangs into the frame.
Camera    A slow push through the fog to the great post at the right of the entrance, ending close on the word
          CROATOAN cut into the stripped wood (zoom 1.0 -> 4.0); the moss and foreground grass slide out of frame.
Animation A crow takes off from the palisade (1.6 s); moss sways; fog drifts in three layers; a cold light finds the letters.
Narration 0.12-4.29  "An entire colony vanished... and left behind one mysterious word."
Text      AN ILLUSTRATED RECONSTRUCTION / The Lost Colony (blackletter)
Sound     Drone and a single viol note out of silence; wind in the pines; the crow; a soft hit on "word".
Out       INK SPREAD (3.6-4.4).

SCENE 2 - THE ARRIVAL                                         4.0 - 11.0 s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2a 4.0-7.4  Open sea: the flagship (an Elizabethan galleon, St George's cross on the mainsail) rolls through an
          engraved sea; the flyboat and the pinnace further out; a low wooded island slides in on the horizon; gulls.
          Camera trucks with the ships; sails billow, pennants stream, spray breaks at the bow.
2b 7.4-11.0 (dissolve) The beach of Roanoke Island: the ship's boat run up on the sand, foam washing along the
          water's edge; colonists walk up the beach with a chest, a sack, a basket, a musket; a woman, children;
          one man still stepping out of the boat. Forest edge with dunes and palmetto behind (parallax).
Narration 4.40-10.65 "July, 1587. More than a hundred English men, women and children landed on Roanoke Island."
Text      JULY 1587 / ROANOKE ISLAND - OFF THE COAST OF PRESENT-DAY NORTH CAROLINA
Sound     Waves, wind in the sails, creaking hull, gulls; the theme begins (recorder, lute, viol); footsteps in sand.
Out       MOVING FOREGROUND WIPE (10.6-11.4): a dark timber sweeps across the lens.

SCENE 3 - BUILDING A NEW LIFE                                 11.0 - 18.0 s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3a 11.0-13.75 A clearing in the forest: three timber-framed cottages at different stages; posts rise, rafters go up,
          wattle and daub fill the walls, thatch is laid course by course. Men drive pegs with mallets; a man
          carries a log; a woman with a basket and a child cross; barrels, a crate and sacks; a campfire and chimney smoke.
3b 13.75-18.0 (dissolve) Greyer: a woman scrapes the bottom of an empty barrel; a slumped sack; a sentry in a morion
          helmet with a matchlock stands by the new palisade looking into the forest - an arrow strikes the post
          beside him (16.55) and he turns. (The arrow stands for George Howe's death; no attacker is shown.)
Narration 11.35-17.73 "They rebuilt an abandoned fort's houses. But food was short, and clashes with local peoples turned deadly."
Text      SUPPLIES RAN SHORT
Sound     Mallets on timber, the fire; the music thins to a drone; wind; the arrow's whistle and thunk.
Out       PAGE TURN (17.55-18.45).

SCENE 4 - THE DEPARTURE                                       18.0 - 25.0 s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4a 18.0-21.6 Medium shot on the beach: John White (grey beard, ruff, capotain hat) reaches for his daughter Eleanor's
          hand; she holds the newborn Virginia; Ananias Dare beside them; behind, the ship waits and a sailor stands
          by the boat. Slow push in.
4b 21.6-25.0 (dissolve) Evening: the settlers stand on the beach watching, one waving; Eleanor and the baby in the
          foreground. The ship sails out, shrinks, and sinks below the horizon hull first, then the sails.
Narration 18.35-24.17 "Governor John White sailed to England for supplies... leaving his newborn granddaughter, Virginia Dare."
Text      JOHN WHITE - GOVERNOR OF THE COLONY / VIRGINIA DARE - HIS GRANDDAUGHTER, BORN 18 AUGUST 1587
Sound     Surf; the air passes to the viol; a soft choir as the ship goes.
Out       PAPER TEAR (24.6-25.4).

SCENE 7 - THE CLUE                                            40.0 - 48.0 s
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7a 40.0-42.3 Inside the palisade: tall grass, the posts of houses taken down, dug-up chests broken open, torn pages,
          rusted armour. White walks in and stops, looking round; two sailors follow.
7b 42.3-48.0 (dissolve) The great post: White in the foreground, from behind, raising his hand; a shaft of light falls
          across the word; the camera pushes in until CROATOAN fills the frame. On "no cross" a dashed burgundy cross
          is sketched over the letters and fades - the sign that was not there.
Narration 40.35-47.10 "The houses were gone. Carved into a post, one word: CROATOAN. And no cross, their agreed sign of distress."
Text      NO CROSS: NO SIGN OF DISTRESS
Sound     Wind, a creaking timber, a low drone; a heartbeat; a deep hit on CROATOAN; a thin shimmer for the cross.
Out       ANIMATED MAP TRANSITION (47.5-48.5): the picture shrinks, framed in ink, into the map.
