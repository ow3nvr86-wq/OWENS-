THE LOST COLONY OF ROANOKE
A 60-second vertical dark-history Short in 2D illustrated animation, made entirely with code
===========================================================================================

1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 59.8 s, H.264 + AAC. In July 1587 more than a hundred English men, women and children
landed on Roanoke Island. Their governor, John White, sailed to England for supplies, leaving his newborn
granddaughter Virginia Dare; war with Spain kept him away for three years. When he came back in August 1590 the
houses were gone, and one word was carved into a post: CROATOAN, with no cross, the agreed sign of distress. The
video sets out the theories, labelled as theories, and ends on the open question.

2D ONLY. There is no 3D anywhere: no Blender, no 3D models, no 3D renderer or camera. Every frame is drawn with
pycairo as flat vector shapes with an ink outline, cel shadows and engraving hatches; numpy adds paper, grain, fog
and the colour grade; PIL draws the captions; ffmpeg encodes. Same engine and look as Dancing_Plague_1518/.

THE 2D TECHNIQUES
-----------------
- Characters: a 2D puppet rig per character (body, limbs, hands, shoes, gowns, doublets, ruffs, capotain hats,
  morion helmets, knitted caps, faces with eyes, lids, brows, nose and mouth) driven by pose functions: walk cycles,
  idles with breathing and blinking, head turns, reactions, hammering, carrying a chest, a log, a sack or a baby,
  rowing, waving, raising a trumpet or a lantern. Animated on twos (15 drawings a second) with line boil.
- Backgrounds: skies and forest painted once into layers; a multiplane camera moves and scales each layer by a
  different amount (sky 0.08, far pines 0.3, near woods 0.6, the action plane 1.0, foreground 1.25-1.5).
  The planes the camera pushes into (the palisade and post, houses, ships, sea) are drawn live every frame,
  so the carved word stays sharp at a 6x zoom.
- Environment: an engraved sea whose wave rows drift at their own speeds with parallax, rolling ships with
  billowing sails and streaming pennants, bow spray and foam, swaying moss and grass, drifting fog, smoke, a
  crow taking off, gulls, falling leaves, snow, rain and lightning, a moonlit night with a fire glowing through
  the trees, shafts of dawn light, flames on the fire ships.
- Transitions: ink spread, moving foreground timber wipes, cross dissolves, a page turn, a paper tear, an
  animated map transition (the post shrinks into the chart) and a fade to black.
- Finishing: an aged-paper texture multiplied over every frame (shifted every second frame), a palette grade that
  darkens with the story, grain and vignette.


HOW TO RUN IT
-------------
Requirements: Python 3 with numpy, scipy, pillow and pycairo; ffmpeg. The voice needs kokoro-onnx with
kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin. In this repository's cloud sessions the SessionStart hook installs
the shared tools and exports $SHORTS_PY and $KOKORO_PY (pycairo: $SHORTS_PY -m pip install pycairo).

  cd Lost_Colony_Roanoke
  $KOKORO_PY tools/voiceover.py              # narration.wav, subtitles.srt, script.txt, build/vo_timing.json
  $SHORTS_PY src/sets.py                     # background layers and the map (about 40 s)
  $SHORTS_PY -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'src'); import post; post.save_paper()"
  $SHORTS_PY audio.py                        # music, ambience, effects, mixes, sound_design.txt
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --stills 0.5,9,20,44  # look-dev stills -> build/stills/
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --preview             # -> renders/preview.mp4 (about 4 minutes)
  $SHORTS_PY render.py                       # -> final_video.mp4 (about 12 minutes on 4 cores)
  $SHORTS_PY render.py --no-voice            # -> renders/final_video_no_voice.mp4
  $SHORTS_PY tools/export_assets.py          # character and prop sheets


ACCURACY
--------
- Facts are checked in sources.txt (section 4), mainly against John White's own 1590 account (printed by
  Hakluyt), the National Park Service (Fort Raleigh National Historic Site) and NCpedia.
- Numbers said with care: "more than a hundred" colonists (sources give 112-121).
- The brief's opening line was softened from "all they left behind was one word" to "left behind one mysterious
  word", because White also found chests, the palisade and "CRO" on a tree.
- The colonists' fate is never stated: moving to Croatoan and joining Native communities are labelled THEORY, the
  digs at Hatteras and in Bertie County are shown as searches, and the map is stamped NOT PROVEN.
- Artistic reconstruction: all faces and figures are invented, the houses, palisade and letter forms are
  plausible reconstructions, the map is an illustration, the seasons are symbolic. Native people appear only as
  towns on the map; they are not given invented likenesses.
