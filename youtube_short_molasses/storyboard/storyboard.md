# Scene-by-scene storyboard

The format is 1080×1920, 30 fps, 1,778 frames (59.3 s). Every cut is derived from the measured voiceover timing (`CUTS` in `scripts/animation.py`). "Sim t" is the time in seconds since the tank failed, in the shallow-water simulation that drives the molasses.

**Visual identity**
- Palette: winter-noon steel blues and brick reds, the molasses as a near-black amber, and sodium-amber highlights for every number.
- Captions: Anton, white with a black outline, with the spoken word lit amber.
- Period details: 1919 date and year tags set in Old Standard on newsprint-cream.
- Grade: slight S-curve, warm highlights and cool shadows, vignette and film grain throughout.
