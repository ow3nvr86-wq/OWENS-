WHAT IF MONEY GREW ON TREES? - OPENING HOOK OPTIONS
=====================================================

Scored 1-5 on each criterion from the brief (curiosity, visual potential, originality, comedy, open loop).

OPTION 1 (anti-hook, contradiction)
  "By noon, everyone on Earth was a millionaire. By dinner, nobody could afford a sandwich."
  Curiosity 5 | Visual 5 | Originality 4 | Comedy 5 | Open loop 5   = 24/25
  - Drops the viewer into the middle of the situation with a paradox: everyone is rich AND everyone is broke.
  - The picture writes itself and lands in under two seconds: a grinning man hugging a mountain of cash walks up to a
    till, and one sandwich costs $1,999,999.99. Works with the sound off.
  - The open loop is the exact question the video answers: how can everyone become rich and still be broke?
  - Its timing structure (noon / dinner) promises a fast, escalating story.

OPTION 2 (event hook)
  "The first guy picked $100 off a tree. Three hours later, the entire city was fighting over them."
  Curiosity 4 | Visual 4 | Originality 3 | Comedy 3 | Open loop 3   = 17/25
  - Strong image, but it explains the premise immediately, and "people fight over money" is the expected outcome;
    it doesn't set up the twist (the money becoming worthless). Used instead as the start of Scene 2.

OPTION 3 (imagine hook)
  "Imagine waking up and discovering that your backyard tree is worth more than your house."
  Curiosity 3 | Visual 3 | Originality 2 | Comedy 2 | Open loop 2   = 12/25
  - "Imagine..." is a slow, familiar opener; it asks the viewer to do the work, and the premise points to "you get
    rich", not to the payoff. Its best idea (the backyard tree) survives as the "MY FIRST MONEY TREE" gag.

SELECTED: OPTION 1.
It is the only hook that is funny, visual and paradoxical at the same time, and its contradiction is resolved by the
ending (more money, same amount of stuff -> higher prices), so the payoff keeps the opening's promise. The last shot
returns to the same price card, framed exactly like the opening, so the Short loops.
