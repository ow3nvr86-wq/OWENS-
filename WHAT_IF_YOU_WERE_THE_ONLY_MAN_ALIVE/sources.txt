2. CHECKED BUT NOT USED
-----------------------
- Hook 3's "4 billion women": the UN's World Population Prospects 2024 puts the world population at about 8.2 billion
  in 2024 (UN press release, July 2024), and women are just under half of it - about 49.7-49.8 % (Statistics Times,
  from UN WPP data; World Bank indicator "Population, female (% of total population)"), i.e. roughly 4.0-4.1 billion.
  "About 4 billion" would be accurate, but the brief selected Hook 2, so no number is used in the film.
