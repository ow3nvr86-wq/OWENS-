WHAT IF YOU WERE THE ONLY MAN ALIVE? - OPENING HOOKS AND ENDING
================================================================

The three hooks come from the brief; the brief selected Hook 2. Scores are this production's own assessment.

HOOK 1  "You wake up tomorrow, and you're the only man left on Earth."
        Clear and personal, but "tomorrow" is a set-up word: it describes the premise instead of dropping the viewer
        into it.                                                                                     Score 20/25
HOOK 2  "Every man on Earth has disappeared. Except you."                                            (SELECTED)
        A shock statement, then a two-word twist that puts the viewer at the centre. In the film it is folded into the
        first line ("You wake up, and every man on Earth has disappeared. Except you."), with the picture opening on an
        extreme close-up of his eyes - no introduction - and a hard musical hit on "Except you."    Score 24/25
HOOK 3  "Imagine being the last man alive... while 4 billion women are still here."
        Strong curiosity, but "imagine" is an introduction, and the number invites the wrong kind of joke and a
        fact-check (about 4 billion is roughly right - see sources.txt - but the brief chose not to use it).   Score 19/25

RETENTION
- First frame: his eyes opening (no titles, no intro); the BREAKING NEWS alert on his phone by 1.5 s.
- Fast cuts in the first 10 s: a new shot every 1.0-1.5 s (bedroom, phone, TV, square, newsroom, his face on the news).
- Stings and whooshes on the news cuts; the music grows as the world finds out.
- The twist at 18 s ("The world doesn't stop.") flips the music and the colour from tense to bright and ordinary.
- Strong visual payoff: THE LAST MAN ALIVE on a giant screen across the street, behind him, as the camera pushes into his
  eyes. The last frame is his eyes closing; the first frame is his eyes opening, so the Short loops.

ENDING
The brief's last line is used as written: "You're not suddenly in charge of the planet. You're just the biggest mystery
on it." It lands on the payoff shot; the music stops on the last word and a single news-alert chime plays as his eyes
close, leading straight back into the alarm at the start.
