WHAT IF YOU WERE THE ONLY MAN ALIVE? - SOUND DESIGN, MUSIC AND TIMING
============================================================

Everything below is synthesised by code in animation.py --stage audio (numpy only: oscillators, filtered noise,
envelopes, convolution reverb). No samples, recordings, stock sound or copyrighted music. The narration is a
synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M, voice bm_george) made by tools/voiceover.py. Music, ambience and effects duck under
the voice so the narration always stays on top.

THE IDEA: a breaking-news story that turns out not to be a disaster. A sleepy morning pad is cut dead by an impact on
"Except you."; a driving news pulse with stings and whooshes builds with every cut as the world finds out; the search
adds drums, tremolo strings, helicopter rotors and camera shutters; then the twist - "The world doesn't stop." -
flips the music to something light and ordinary (marimba and claps) over traffic, office chatter, a pager, a gavel
and a train. A warm swell carries the last line and stops on the last word, leaving one soft alert chime as his eyes
close (the film loops to the alarm).

Files: audio/mix_with_voice.wav (final mix, -14 LUFS), audio/mix_no_voice.wav (music + sound only, -14 LUFS),
audio/narration.wav (voice only).

MUSIC GUIDE (if you re-score it in CapCut or another editor)
------------------------------------------------------------
- 0-3 s: soft and sleepy; a hard hit on "Except you." (3.0 s).
- 4-10 s: news-channel energy - a driving minor-key pulse (120-130 bpm), stings on the TV and on "the only man alive".
- 10-18 s: tension rising - add drums and strings; helicopter and press-pack sound effects.
- 18 s: switch to something light, bright and everyday for "The world doesn't stop." (the joke of the twist).
- 25-29 s: one warm swell under the last line; stop on the last word.
- Keep the narration 10-15 dB above the music; YouTube Shorts plays back at about -14 LUFS.
- Use only music you have the rights to (your own, a licensed library, or YouTube Audio Library tracks).
