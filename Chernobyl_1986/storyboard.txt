CHERNOBYL: THE NIGHT THAT CHANGED HISTORY - SCENE-BY-SCENE STORYBOARD
=====================================================================

59.8 s, 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 fps. 2D only: every frame is drawn with pycairo vector ink (render.py, src/).
Characters are animated on twos with line boil; camera, smoke, fire, lights, water, traffic and particles move on
every frame. Depth comes only from flat layers moved at different speeds (a multiplane camera).
Captions: bold Cinzel capitals in parchment with a dark outline; key words on a burgundy ink plate.

SCENE 1 - THE NIGHT EVERYTHING CHANGED                         0.0 - 5.0 s
  Night across the cooling pond: the plant lit up, its stack's red lights blinking, Pripyat's windows on the horizon
  at the left, pylons, the lights reflected in the water. A slow push toward Reactor 4. At 4.15 s a flash; a ragged
  fireball, debris flung in arcs, a ring of light, a column of lit smoke; the camera shakes.
  Narration 0.30-5.08 "At 1:23 in the morning, a nuclear reactor in Soviet Ukraine... was destroyed."
  Text: CHERNOBYL NUCLEAR POWER PLANT, SOVIET UKRAINE / 26 APRIL 1986, 01:23
  Sound: industrial hum, crickets, frogs, water; a rumble builds; a huge low impact.
  Out: WHITE FLASH (4.65-5.35).

  parallax planes; trees sway in the foreground.
  Narration 5.45-10.78 "A few kilometres away, tens of thousands of people lived ordinary lives in the city of Pripyat."
  Text: PRIPYAT / THE PLANT'S CITY, A FEW KILOMETRES AWAY
  Sound: birds, traffic, voices, a bicycle bell; a gentle piano theme over a viol.
  Out: MOVING FOREGROUND WIPE (10.58-11.42).

  Narration 11.40-17.84 "That night, during a safety test on Reactor 4, a flawed design and operating errors

SCENE 4 - THE EXPLOSION                                        18.0 - 25.0 s
  The shattered reactor building at night: broken walls and beams, the glowing interior, two columns of smoke lit
  from below rising and leaning in the wind, fires on the turbine-hall roof, embers drifting, the stack, pylons,
  blue emergency lights flashing at its foot. Slow push. No people are shown hurt.
  Narration 18.35-24.29 "Two explosions tore the reactor open. The fire released radioactive material into the air."
  Text: REACTOR 4 / TWO EXPLOSIONS, THEN FIRE
  Sound: fire roar and crackle, wind, a drone and low choir; a siren far off.
  Out: CROSS DISSOLVE (24.55-25.45).

SCENE 5 - THE FIREFIGHTERS                                     25.0 - 32.0 s
  Fire engines (red, white stripe, ladders, blue beacons) arrive under sweeping searchlights; one brakes into place;
  firefighters in canvas suits and helmets run to the building, two hold a spraying hose, one directs; hose lines
  across the ground; the burning plant behind. The camera tracks with the arriving truck.
  Narration 25.35-31.75 "Firefighters and plant workers rushed in, facing an emergency whose full scale was not yet understood."
  Text: THE FIRST FIRE CREWS ARRIVED WITHIN MINUTES
  Sound: sirens, engines, air brakes, water; a respectful low choir and viol.
  Out: PAGE TURN (31.55-32.45).

  Out: MOVING FOREGROUND WIPE (38.58-39.42).

  another pulls away and the camera follows the column out.
  Narration 39.35-45.77 "About thirty-six hours after the explosion, the buses came. Residents were told the evacuation was temporary."
