CHERNOBYL: THE NIGHT THAT CHANGED HISTORY - SOUND DESIGN TIMELINE
============================================================

Everything is synthesised in audio.py with numpy: no samples, no recordings, no third-party audio.
Music: an original, restrained piano theme in A minor over viol and drones. It is gentle for the ordinary city,
gives way to a quickening pulse and alarms in the control room, stops at the surge, becomes a low choir for the
fire crews, returns as single notes in the empty city, and ends on one A minor chord.
Mix: music and ambience are ducked about 7 dB under the narration; both mixes are normalised to -14 LUFS (true peak -1.5 dB).

    0.30 -   5.08  At 1:23 in the morning, a nuclear reactor in Soviet Ukraine... was destroyed.
    5.45 -  10.78  A few kilometres away, tens of thousands of people lived ordinary lives in the city of Pripyat.
   11.40 -  17.84  That night, during a safety test on Reactor 4, a flawed design and operating errors combined... and the power surged.
   18.35 -  24.29  Two explosions tore the reactor open. The fire released radioactive material into the air.
   25.35 -  31.75  Firefighters and plant workers rushed in, facing an emergency whose full scale was not yet understood.
   32.35 -  38.51  That Saturday, life in Pripyat carried on. Many residents had little idea of the danger nearby.
   39.35 -  45.77  About thirty-six hours after the explosion, the buses came. Residents were told the evacuation was temporary.
   46.35 -  52.78  They never came back. In 1986 alone, about 116,000 people were evacuated.
   53.30 -  58.38  Its consequences are still felt today, and it changed how the world sees nuclear safety.
