He beat a Cyclops, a witch and the land of the dead. But the real monster... was him.
Troy has fallen. Odysseus just wants to go home.
A Cyclops eats his men. Giants sink his ships. A witch turns his crew into pigs. He visits the dead, slips past the Sirens, and his starving men eat a god's cattle. A storm kills them all.
A nymph feeds him lotus. He forgets home for seven years.
Back home, suitors want his throne. He returns in disguise, strings the bow no one else can... and the suitors pay.
The twist? The raiders from the sea everyone feared were the Greeks. They were him.
